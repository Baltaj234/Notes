# Mitigating AI risks

AI presents numerous opportunities for innovation and efficiency, but also comes with significant risks that need to be carefully managed.

One of the primary concerns is that AI systems can make decisions that are difficult to interpret, leading to a lack of trasperancy and accountability.

This can result in harmful outcomes, such as biased decisions or privacy violations.

To mitigate these risks it is important to have human oversight and ensure transperancy in the AI use process.

# What is responsible AI?
Responsible AI is the approach to developing, assessing, and deploying artficial intelligent systems in a safe, trustworthy, and ethical way. AI systems are the product of many decisions made by the people who develop and deploy them.

# Microsoft and Github's 6 principles of responsible AI

1. Fairness: AI systems should treat all people fairly
2. reliability and safety: AI systems should perform reliably and safely
3. Privacy and Security: AI systems should be secure and respect privacy
4. Inclusiveness: AI systems should empower everyone and engage people
5. Transperancy: AI systems should be understandable
6. Accountability: People should be accountable for AI systems
