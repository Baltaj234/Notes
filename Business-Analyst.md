# What is a business Analyst?

Business analyst help guide businesses in improving processes, products, services, and software through data analysis. These agile workers straddle the line between IT and the business to help bridge the gap and improve efficiency.

They engage with leaders and users to understand how data-driven changes to processes, products, services, software and hardware can improve efficiencies and add value. 

Business Analyst's may work with data sets to improve products, hardware, tools, software, services or process.

# Typical Responsiblities

Budgeting and forecasting
Planning and monitoring
Variance analysis
Pricing
Reporting
Defining business requirements and reporting them back to stakeholders

Tasked with prioritizing technical and functional requirements., identifying what clients want, determining what is feasible to deliver. Requiring a deep understanding of systems, how they function, who will need to be involved, and necessary steops to get everyone on board.
